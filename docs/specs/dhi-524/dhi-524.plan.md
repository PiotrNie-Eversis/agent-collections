**Success Measure**: After `node adapters/cursor/generate.mjs`, seven mapped `.mdc` files are regular files (not symlinks) with correct front matter + GENERATED marker; BA symlinks and local `eversis-project-stack.mdc` untouched; second re-run is idempotent; review has no BLOCKER vs Jira AC.

**Do NOT touch / do NOT add**:

- Skills/prompts materialization (Q7=A)
- Generating/overwriting `eversis-project-stack.mdc` (Q4=A)
- Removing BA symlinks (Q3=B — keep)

- Edits to `cursor-collections`; committing `docs/specs/dhi-*`

**Output shape:** front matter → `<!-- GENERATED by adapters/cursor/generate.mjs — do not edit; re-run the generator -->` → core body.
